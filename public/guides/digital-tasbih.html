Digital tasbih: a guide to counting dhikr on your phone

A digital tasbih is a phone, watch, or web-based counter used to keep track of recitations. A clear counter can make it easier to notice your count when you choose to count, without turning a personal act of remembrance into a competition or a requirement.

How does a digital tasbih work?

Most digital tasbih tools add one to a visible count each time you tap a button. Many let you choose a target, reset or undo a count, or review your own session history. A web app can run in a mobile browser or, on a supported device, be installed as a progressive web app.

Choosing a dhikr counter

  • Simple, readable counting: look for a large, easy-to-tap control and a count that remains visible.
  • Useful personal controls: an undo option, an optional target, and straightforward session controls can help you use the counter your way.
  • Privacy that is explained: check whether records stay on your device, whether a user account is required, and whether optional services transmit information.
  • Honest offline details: an offline-capable app generally needs to be loaded and cached before it can be used offline. Features that use a server still need a connection.
  • Comfort and accessibility: consider readable text, keyboard support where applicable, optional sound or vibration, and your own device's accessibility settings.

Using Dhikr Counter as a digital tasbih

  1. Open the app's home screen and select a remembrance from the dhikr library.
  2. Review the available text and meaning, and check religious wording or translations with a source you trust when needed.
  3. Set a personal target if it is useful to you. It is optional and does not represent religious advice.
  4. Tap the counter to keep count. Sound and vibration are optional and can be changed in the app settings.
  5. Review or export your device-stored history if you wish. The app does not automatically sync these records to an account.

Can a phone dhikr counter work offline?

Dhikr Counter is an installable, offline-capable web app. Once its web app and needed resources have loaded and been cached, supported local counter features can work without a live connection. Online features, including AI Talk and remote push messaging, still require the relevant online service. Do not assume a freshly opened, uncached site will work offline.

Keep counting personal

A counter is only a counting aid. Choose your own pace and use optional targets or statistics only if they are helpful. A counter cannot assess intention, the religious validity of worship, or the spiritual value of a remembrance. For religious guidance, consult trusted primary sources and a qualified scholar.

Dhikr Counter is a software tool, not a religious authority. Optional AI answers should not be relied on for Islamic rulings.
