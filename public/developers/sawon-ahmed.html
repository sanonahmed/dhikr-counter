The person behind the project

Sawon Ahmed, developer of Dhikr Counter

Sawon Ahmed is the developer of Dhikr Counter, a free digital tasbih and dhikr app designed to make personal remembrance easier to count and organize on the web.

About Sawon Ahmed

Sawon Ahmed develops Dhikr Counter, a browser-based tool for keeping track of dhikr, durood, istighfar, and tasbih. The project brings a digital counter together with a library of remembrance entries and optional features for personal use.

The app provides an English and Bengali interface, personal goals and reminders, session history, and an installable progressive web app experience. Its local-first design keeps personal counting records in the user's browser rather than requiring an account or automatic cloud sync.

About the Dhikr Counter project

Dhikr Counter is a free Islamic dhikr counter and digital tasbih developed by Sawon Ahmed. People can choose an entry, count at their own pace, set optional personal targets, and review their own history. The app also includes tools for collections, customization, backups, and optional reminders.

As an installable web app, Dhikr Counter can support offline use after the app and its required resources have loaded and been cached. Features that depend on online services still require a connection. The app is a counting aid, not a religious authority or a substitute for trusted Islamic sources and qualified scholarship.

Project approach

The project combines a straightforward digital tasbih with practical controls for privacy, accessibility, and personal organization. English and Bengali language options help make the interface useful to more people, while optional goals and statistics are presented as personal tracking tools—not as measures of religious worth.
