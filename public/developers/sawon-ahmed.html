About the developer

Building a thoughtful digital tasbih

Sawon Ahmed develops Dhikr Counter, a browser-based tool for keeping track of dhikr, durood, istighfar, and tasbih. The project brings a clear counting experience together with a library of remembrance entries and practical tools for personal use.

The app reflects a product-focused approach to software development: its counter, saved sessions, goals, reminders, and customization are brought together in one installable web app. An English and Bengali interface makes the experience available in both languages.

Product & technology

A practical web app, shaped around its users

Dhikr Counter is built as a modern web application with React and TypeScript, and is designed as an installable progressive web app. It combines local data storage with a responsive interface, so core counting tools can remain available offline after the app and its resources have been loaded and cached.

The project keeps its purpose clear: it is a personal counting and organization tool, not a religious authority. Optional counts, reminders, and statistics are personal features—not measures of faith or religious worth.
