Your device, your data

Dhikr Counter privacy

The core counter is local-first: your personal counts are stored in this browser on this device. No account, advertising profile, or third-party analytics is required to use the counter.

Last updated: 23 November 2026. This page describes the app's current behavior. Hosting providers and services you choose to use may process technical data under their own policies.

Information stored on your device

Your dhikr and Salah records, session history, custom entries, collections, goals, reminders, and preferences are stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB. The app does not automatically send these personal records to an account or cloud-sync service. If you clear site data or lose the device, local records may be lost.

Backups you choose to make

If you export a backup, your browser downloads a file to your device. You choose where to keep, share, or delete that file. If you import a backup, its supported records are written to your local app database.

Network requests and third-party services

Like any website, visiting the app requires a connection to its hosting provider. The app also loads its selected typography from Google Fonts, which means the browser may request font files from Google. Standard web hosting and network infrastructure may process technical request information such as an IP address.

Optional AI Talk: if enabled and used, the text you send in an AI Talk conversation is sent through the app server to its configured AI provider to generate a reply. Do not submit sensitive personal information. This feature may not be configured on every deployment. The app’s owner-operated AI service may apply rate limits.

Optional notifications: if you enable push notifications, your browser creates a push subscription, which the app server stores to deliver messages. You can revoke notification permission in your browser or device settings. Local reminder scheduling and server push delivery are separate features.

Offline use

After the app and its resources have loaded and been cached, the progressive web app can work offline for supported local features. Features that need an online service, such as AI Talk or delivering a remote push message, need a network connection.

Your controls

You can clear Dhikr Counter's site data in your browser to erase locally stored records. You can first use the app’s backup tools to download your own copy. Revoke notification permissions separately in device or browser settings, and avoid using optional connected services if you do not wish to send them information.

Children and accounts

The public counter does not require registration or a user profile. There is no in-app advertising or behavioral advertising analytics.

Questions and changes

This is an informational privacy summary for the application, not a promise that hosting providers or optional third-party services never process technical data. Material changes in app behavior should be reflected here.

Avoid entering private, health, or other sensitive information into optional AI conversations. Dhikr Counter is not a religious authority or professional advice service.
