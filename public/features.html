A quieter way to keep count

A free dhikr counter and digital tasbih

Dhikr Counter is a simple Islamic remembrance companion for counting dhikr, durood, istighfar, and tasbih. Open it in your browser, choose a remembrance, and count at your own pace—without creating an account.

Open the free counter

Digital tasbih counter

Count with a large tap-friendly counter, set a personal target, and continue or reset a session as needed. Sound and vibration feedback are optional.

Dhikr, durood, and istighfar library

Browse a built-in library of remembrance entries with Arabic text, transliteration, and translations where available. Create your own personal dhikr entries too.

English and Bengali

Switch the app interface between English and Bengali. Bengali-language interface support does not replace checking a religious text or translation with a trusted source.

Private, on-device history

Counting sessions, custom entries, goals, and settings are stored in your browser on this device. There is no account or automatic cloud sync.

Offline-ready PWA

After the app has loaded and its resources are cached, the progressive web app can continue working offline. Install it from a supported browser for a convenient app-like experience.

Goals, reminders, and reflection

Set personal counting goals, review your own session statistics, and use optional reminders. The personal Salah tracker records your own check-ins; it does not evaluate religious validity.

Backup and restore

Export your locally stored app data and restore a previous backup. Keep your downloaded backup somewhere safe; the app does not sync it automatically.

Optional AI Talk

When the service is configured, AI Talk can answer conversational questions about prayer and dhikr. It is not a scholar or religious authority; do not treat its answers as a fatwa.

Designed for personal remembrance

Dhikr Counter is a tool, not a source of religious rulings. It makes no claim that a particular target, streak, or app feature carries a religious reward. For questions of worship or Islamic law, consult a trusted qualified scholar and reliable primary sources.

Start counting

Choose a remembrance from the home screen and begin. Your counters and app history stay on this browser unless you choose to export a backup or use an optional connected feature.
